Old Government House Summary Old Government House is one of Auckland's most historically significant buildings and predates the University itself. Completed in 1856, it served as the official residence of New Zealand's governors while Auckland was the colonial capital and later functioned as the city's vice-regal residence for more than a century. It was designed by prominent architect William Mason and is widely regarded as one of the country's most important surviving nineteenth-century government buildings. Since being transferred to the University of Auckland in 1969, Old Government House has served as the Staff Common Room and a venue for lectures, receptions, dinners, and special events. Set among mature gardens and historic trees, the building offers a rare glimpse into New Zealand's colonial and constitutional history. Royal visitors stayed there several times, and Queen Elizabeth II delivered her 1953 Christmas message to the Commonwealth from the house.
