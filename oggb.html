Owen G Glenn Building (OGGB) Summary The Sir Owen G Glenn Building is the home of the University of Auckland Business School and is one of the most recognisable contemporary buildings on campus. Completed in 2008, the purpose-built facility was funded in part by a landmark donation from businessman Sir Owen G Glenn and was designed to bring together teaching, research, industry engagement, and executive education under one roof. The building is centred around a dramatic 26-metre-high atrium and contains lecture theatres, case-study rooms, computer laboratories, collaborative learning spaces, and the BNZ Financial Trading Room. Its modern architecture contrasts sharply with the nearby ClockTower, symbolising the University's blend of heritage and innovation. The building has received numerous architectural awards and remains a focal point for business education, industry partnerships, and major events. Quick Facts Opened: February 2008 Home of the University of Auckland Business School Features a 26-metre-high central atrium Located at 12 Grafton Road Named after Sir Owen G Glenn following a major donation Contains lecture theatres, trading room, computer labs and event spaces Winner of multiple architecture awards and shortlisted at the World Architecture Festival
