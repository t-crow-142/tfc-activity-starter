Welcome to Waipapa Taumata Rau | University of Auckland

Located in the heart of New Zealand's largest city, Waipapa Taumata Rau | University of Auckland is New Zealand's leading university and a vibrant centre for learning, research, innovation, and community engagement. The City Campus brings together historic landmarks and contemporary facilities, creating a unique environment where heritage, education, and innovation intersect.

From iconic heritage buildings to world-class recreational and teaching spaces, the campus reflects the University's rich history and forward-looking vision. Explore four of the University's most significant sites and discover the stories, people, and purpose that have shaped the institution for generations.

The ClockTower

The ClockTower is the University of Auckland's most iconic landmark and a defining feature of the City Campus. Opened in 1926 as the former Arts Building, its distinctive Gothic-inspired architecture and prominent central location have made it a symbol of the University for nearly a century.

Today, the ClockTower houses key leadership and ceremonial spaces, including the Vice-Chancellor's Office, while continuing to serve as one of Auckland's most recognised heritage buildings.

Hiwa Recreation Centre

Hiwa Recreation Centre is the University's state-of-the-art wellbeing, recreation, and fitness hub. Opened in 2024, it provides students, staff, and the wider community with access to modern sporting, aquatic, and wellness facilities designed to support physical, social, and mental wellbeing.

Featuring a swimming pool, sports courts, fitness studios, rooftop recreation spaces, and innovative training facilities, Hiwa has quickly become a vibrant centre of campus life.

Old Government House

Old Government House is one of New Zealand's most historically significant buildings. Completed in 1856, it originally served as the residence of New Zealand's governors and played an important role in the country's early colonial administration.

Since becoming part of the University in 1969, the building has served as a venue for lectures, events, and gatherings while preserving an important connection to Auckland's civic and constitutional history.

Owen G Glenn Building

The Sir Owen G Glenn Building is home to the University of Auckland Business School and stands as one of the campus's most recognisable contemporary buildings. Opened in 2008, it was designed to bring together teaching, research, industry engagement, and executive education within a purpose-built environment.

Featuring a dramatic central atrium, modern learning spaces, lecture theatres, and collaborative study areas, the building serves as a hub for business education and innovation at the University.
