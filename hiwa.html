Hiwa Recreation Centre Summary Hiwa is the University of Auckland's state-of-the-art recreation and wellness centre, opened in 2024 as the flagship facility for student wellbeing, sport, and recreation. Located on Symonds Street at the heart of the City Campus, it was designed to support not only physical fitness but also social connection and mental wellbeing. The project was created by Warren and Mahoney in association with Canadian sports-facility specialists MJMA. The multi-level facility includes an aquatic hall, sports courts, fitness studios, a weights and cardio centre, squash courts, rooftop recreation spaces, and an indoor running track. Built on a constrained inner-city site, the building uses a vertical design that stacks facilities above one another while connecting them through a central fitness core. Since opening, it has become a major hub of student life and one of the most advanced university recreation facilities in New Zealand. Quick Facts Opened: 2024 Location: 17 Symonds Street Approximately 20,000-22,000 meter squared facility Features the University's first on-campus swimming pool Includes rooftop turf, running track, squash courts, sports halls, and fitness studios Designed by Warren and Mahoney with MJMA Architects Focused on physical, social, and mental wellbeing
